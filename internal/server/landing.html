Capabilities

Everything you need in an AI agent

Mimir combines persistent memory, tool execution, and local inference into one cohesive agent.

🧠

Persistent Memory

Facts, credentials, context - Mimir stores them in the Cortex and recalls them across every session. No more repeating yourself.

⚡

Runs Locally

Powered by Ollama on your own hardware. Your conversations, secrets, and data never touch the cloud.

🔧

Tool Calling

Execute shell commands, edit files, query databases, and manage your workflow - all through natural conversation.

💬

Session History

Every conversation is saved and searchable. Pick up where you left off, or search for something you discussed weeks ago.

🔒

Passwordless Auth

Just enter your email and verify with a 6-digit code. No passwords to remember, no accounts to manage.

🎨

Book-Spine UI

A unique panel-based interface with draggable spines, live cortex, and real-time dashboard. Designed for power users.

How it works

Up and running in three steps

No API keys, no cloud accounts, no subscriptions. Just you and your machine.

1

Deploy Mimir

One Docker Compose command pulls Ollama, SurrealDB, and Mimir. Everything runs on your hardware.

2

Sign in with your email

No passwords. Enter your email, get a 6-digit code, and you're in. First-time users are auto-created.

3

Start talking

Tell Mimir things - it remembers them. Ask it to run tools, manage files, or look up information. Everything lives in the Cortex.
