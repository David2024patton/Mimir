What it does

Built to be the whole shop.

Not just an autocomplete. A framework that plans, builds, tests, deploys, remembers, and gets smarter.

memory🧠

The Cortex - a brain that grows

A neural knowledge graph (neurons, synapses, engrams) on SurrealDB. Every correction becomes a lesson, every lesson becomes memory. Multilingual embeddings, RAG with citations, a forgetting curve that keeps it sharp.

autonomy🎯

Goal mode

Tell it the end goal. It plans, builds, tests, and doesn't stop until it's done - with checkpoints so you stay in control.

any model⚡

Your models, your call

Any provider - OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter - or fully local with Ollama. Small models welcome.

sandbox🐳

Docker-first

Every project gets a Dockerfile. Databases, previews on real ports, deployed anywhere - never "works on my machine."

open🧩

WASM modules

Build modules in any language - Rust, C, Go, whatever. They compile to WebAssembly and just work, safely sandboxed.

verify🛡️

Three auditors, every step

Loki tests it, Forseti audits the code, Heimdall screenshots the UI. If anything fails, it loops back and fixes it - it never moves on broken.

alive💡

It lights up your room

Task done? Your Corsair keyboard pulses red. Final answer? A gold sweep. Mímir syncs its status to your RGB gear, and the UI glows with it. Futuristic, vibrant, alive.

everything else∞

And the rest of the shop: git commits that explain themselves · visual regression testing · CI/CD that reviews your PRs · a marketplace of forkable experiments · voice in and out · offline and air-gapped · real-time collaboration · a dashboard that counts every token

Seventy-one planned features across 88 epics - see the plan on GitHub.
